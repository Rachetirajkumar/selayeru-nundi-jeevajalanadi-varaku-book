Overview

One river. One redemptive story.

This book invites you to see the Bible not as separate pieces but as one flowing river of God's redemption, beginning in Genesis and reaching its fullness in Revelation.

By tracing the relationships between the books of the Bible, the author helps readers understand the great purpose of God's Word and grow deeper in their life of faith.

"Glory to God alone!"
