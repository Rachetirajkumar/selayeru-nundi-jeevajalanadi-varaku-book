Book Overview

One river. One story of redemption.

This book shows the Bible not as separate pieces but as one flowing stream of God's redemption, beginning in Genesis and reaching its fullness in Revelation.

By tracing how the books of Scripture connect, it helps you grasp the great purpose of God's Word and grow in your life of faith.

The 21-Chapter Journey

Scroll and watch the river flow

Seven stages that follow the stream as it grows.

CHAPTERS 1–3

Beginnings

Creation, the fall, and the first glimpse of grace.

CHAPTERS 4–6

The Promise

Babel, one man and one nation, and a promise that keeps flowing.

CHAPTERS 7–9

A People Formed

Bondage, covenant in the wilderness, and crossing the river into the land.

CHAPTERS 10–13

Kings and Prophets

A king, two kingdoms, the prophets' hope, and a voice that broke the silence.

CHAPTERS 14–15

The Word Became Flesh

The day God came among us, and life flowing from the cross.

CHAPTERS 16–19

The Church

The Spirit comes, the wall falls, and many members become one body.

CHAPTERS 20–21

The River of Life

The returning King, and a people chosen not for themselves but for the world.
